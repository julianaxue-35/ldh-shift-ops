FAQ — behaviour team

How the Behaviour page works, from a request arriving to a Done note.

Working your board

Where do requests come from, and where do they land?

Shelter staff send them from the Behaviour task bar on the shelter staff page, and vets and nurses send them from the Behaviour tab of their Send a request card. Vets can also send an animal with its medical note from the Processing, Sick & Injured and Surgery tools. Everything lands in Incoming at the top, unassigned, unless the sender ticked Send straight to the Behaviour TL.

What is the ring and the table at the bottom?

What the Behaviour team has done counts the cases ticked Done: today (the ring fills as today nears a usual working day) and the week, month, quarter and year so far, with the average per working day and the courthold and incident-review totals. Finished cases are removed from the page after 7 days, but the count is kept (only the kind of case and the date, never the animal).

How will I know a new task has arrived?

A red NEW TASK banner appears at the top, the page plays a short three-beep sound, and the browser tab title flashes. The banner stays until you press ×. Browsers keep sound off until the page has been tapped once, so tap anywhere (or the alert sound button at the top) when you start your shift. The same button mutes the sound. Tasks already on the page when you open it do not ring.

How do I assign or move a task?

Enter your initials in the left sidebar first (kept on this device for the day). On a card use Assign to… (from Incoming) or Move to… (from a lane). Cats can go to Behaviour TL or Cat assist; dogs to Behaviour TL or Dog assist. Cats and dogs never cross. Transport (and a location the board does not recognise) can go to any of the three, so check the species before you pick. Every move is recorded with your initials.

How do I give a task to someone else on the team?

Each lane has an add task button. The TL can give a task to an assist, and an assist can give one back to the TL. Choose the animal, the space and pen, the kind of task, the time frame and a reason, and pick who it is for (only the lanes that suit the species are offered).

What do the time frames and red cards mean?

A card shows how long is left. 30–60 min is due an hour after it was sent, within today is due by the end of today, and within 48 hours is due two days after it was sent. Past the time it turns red and says overdue by …. Cards are sorted with the soonest due first. Use the filters at the top to show one kind, one time frame or courthold only.

How do I finish a task?

Press Done, write a note (what you found and the plan; it is required) and confirm. The card leaves your board, and the note, your initials and the time show on that animal’s card on the Shelter board for 24 hours. Done tasks are deleted from the database after 7 days.

What is the “Medical note from vets” button?

When a vet sends an animal from a shift tool, their whole current note is attached to the card. Press the button to read it (you cannot edit it). The moment you tick Done, the note is deleted from the database. If a task is never ticked Done, the note is deleted within 7 days anyway. Copy anything you need into your own records before you tick Done.

Asking the vets

How do I ask the vets for shelter stress medication or a post adoption med pack?

Use the Send a request card at the top. Choose What (Dispense shelter stress medication, Post adoption med pack or Other), type the animal ID, choose the space and pen / detail, write a reason or note, tick Courthold if it applies, enter your initials and press Send to vets. It goes onto the vet board like any other request, so you do not have to switch pages. For Other, say what you need in the note.

The animal is a courthold — does that change anything?

Ticking Courthold marks the request so the vets can see it. A Courthold reassessment task from staff always shows a COURTHOLD badge, and the courthold filter shows only those.
