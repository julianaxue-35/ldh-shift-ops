FAQ — Admission AA

What lands on this page, how to clear it, and what happens to it afterwards.

Your list

What is on this page?

Everything the vets and nurses have asked you to enter in ShelterMate. It is split into four columns: Processing notes and Sick & Injured notes (finished notes from the vets’ shift tools), Medication charts to print, and Tasks from nurses (FIV, microchip, UV, microchip + UV and vaccination updates). A banner and a sound tell you when something new arrives. Tap the sound button once so your browser allows the sound.

How do I clear an item?

Enter it in ShelterMate, then press Entered in ShelterMate ✓ (Done ✓ on a medication chart). The card is deleted from the database straight away. A card with several animals (a litter, say) has a checklist: tick each animal as you enter it, and the card clears when every animal is ticked.

A note is long — how do I copy it into ShelterMate?

On a note card press View notes to read it, Copy to copy the text, or Download Word if the vet attached the Word file. A medication chart card lists the medication lines, with Download Excel chart to print the chart.

What does PRIORITY mean? What goes red?

A vet can mark a note PRIORITY — do first (for example an animal waiting for a foster). Those cards have a red border, sit at the top and are announced in a banner. Every card also has a 24-hour clock: anything left for 24 hours turns red and is counted as overdue at the top of the page.

A card changed after I opened it — what does “updated” mean?

The vet edited the note and sent it again. The same card is updated rather than a new one added, and it shows updated with the time. Check what changed before you enter it. If you already entered the first version, enter the changes.

Mistakes and tidying

I ticked something by mistake.

Open Completed below the list and press Put back (for a multi-animal card, put back just that animal). You have 7 days; type the animal ID in the box to find it.

How long are completed items kept?

Completed cards are kept for 7 days (with the vet’s note, so a note that was not entered can be put back) and then removed for good. Use the box above the list to find an animal ID. Empty list removes them all straight away.

What if the animal ID in the note is wrong or missing?

The vet’s tool will not send a note without an ID. If the ID was corrected after sending, the vet sends it again under the new ID and that makes a new card; the old one stays here until you remove it, so ask the vet which one to use.

Numbers and who is on

What are the rings and totals at the bottom?

What the Admission AA has done counts cards you have completed, and the average per working day this week, month, season and year. The ring fills as today gets close to a usual working day. It is only a look back; nothing here affects your list.

Why does it say who is on duty?

The nurses set who is on shift on the Nurses page, and it shows here so everyone knows who to ask.
